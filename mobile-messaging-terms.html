Mobile Messaging Terms

Last updated: August 15, 2025  ·  Effective: August 15, 2025

Summary: COREPLUS may send you SMS/WhatsApp messages about your courses, orders, and updates — only with your consent. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP or contacting us. Message and data rates may apply.

1. Overview

These Mobile Messaging Terms ("Messaging Terms") govern the mobile messaging communications between COREPLUS ("we", "us", "our") and you ("user", "you"). By opting in to receive mobile messages from us, you agree to these Messaging Terms, which supplement our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

2. Program Description

COREPLUS may send mobile messages via SMS, MMS, or messaging apps (such as WhatsApp) for the following purposes:

  • Order confirmations and receipts for course purchases
  • Course access notifications and enrolment confirmations
  • Reminders about upcoming live sessions, webinars, or events
  • Important updates to your course or account
  • Promotional messages about new courses and services (only with your explicit consent)
  • Responses to your direct enquiries submitted via our website

3. Consent and Opt-In

We will only send you mobile messages if you have provided explicit consent by:

  • Checking a consent box on our website or course registration form
  • Sending us a message and requesting to be added to our messaging list
  • Providing your phone number with explicit acknowledgement that you consent to receive messages

Consent to receive mobile messages is not a condition of purchasing any course or service. You may complete a purchase without consenting to marketing messages.

4. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your activity and the services you have purchased:

  • Transactional messages — sent as needed based on your account activity (e.g. purchase confirmations, course updates)
  • Promotional messages — no more than 4 messages per month for users who have consented to marketing communications

5. Message and Data Rates

Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan. COREPLUS does not charge for messages sent to you, but your carrier may charge for receiving SMS/MMS messages. Please check with your mobile carrier for details about your plan.

6. How to Opt Out

You may opt out of receiving mobile messages from us at any time using any of the following methods:

  • Reply STOP to any SMS message we send you
  • Reply UNSUBSCRIBE to any SMS message we send you
  • Contact us at support@coreplus-tech.ru and request removal
  • Update your communication preferences in your account settings

After opting out, you will receive a final confirmation message. You will no longer receive marketing messages, but we may still send you transactional messages necessary to fulfil services you have purchased (e.g. course access codes, appointment reminders).

To re-subscribe to marketing messages after opting out, text START to our number or contact us directly.

7. Help

If you need assistance with our mobile messaging programme, reply HELP to any message, or contact us at:

  • Email: support@coreplus-tech.ru
  • Website: coreplus-tech.ru

8. Privacy and Data Use

Your phone number and messaging data are processed in accordance with our Privacy Policy. Specifically:

  • We will not share your phone number with third parties for their marketing purposes

  • Message delivery may involve third-party messaging platforms (e.g. Twilio, WhatsApp Business API) which process data under their own privacy policies
  • You may request deletion of your phone number from our systems at any time

9. Supported Carriers

Our SMS messaging programme supports major carriers in Russia, the EU, and internationally. Carrier support may vary by country. We are not responsible for messages not received due to carrier limitations or network issues outside our control.
